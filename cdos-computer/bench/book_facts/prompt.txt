You extract facts from one paragraph of a book. Write only facts that the paragraph itself states.

Write one fact per line, in exactly this form:
subject | RELATION | kind | value | unit

- subject: the thing the fact is about, named as the paragraph names it.
- RELATION: upper-case words joined by _, for example IS_A, DEFINITION, HAS_PART, EQUALS, NAMED_AFTER.
- kind: S for a name or short phrase, N for a number, D for a date, T for a whole sentence.
- value: the value. For kind N, a plain number.
- unit: the unit of a kind N value; otherwise leave it empty.

Write nothing else: no numbering, no headings, no explanations. If the paragraph states no facts, write NONE.

Example
Paragraph: Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius at sea level. It is a compound of hydrogen and oxygen.
water | BOILING_POINT | N | 100 | C
water | IS_A | S | compound |
water | CONTAINS_ELEMENT | S | hydrogen |
water | CONTAINS_ELEMENT | S | oxygen |

Paragraph: {paragraph}
